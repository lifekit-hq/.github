# LifeKit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-15

LifeKit is a self-hosted personal software stack built for a single owner. It is not a public service, and it has no other users.

## What the Google app accesses

The LifeKit Google app is connected only to the owner's own Google account. It accesses the owner's own Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Tasks. It does this so the owner's own agents can read that data and, when the owner asks, send mail on the owner's behalf.

## Where data goes

Data fetched from Google stays on the owner's own server. LifeKit does not sell data and does not share it with third parties. The one exception is the AI model provider, which processes the requests the owner's agents send to it, and that can include content from the owner's Google data.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google account under Security > Third-party access (or "Your connections to third-party apps and services"). Removing LifeKit there ends its access immediately.

## Contact

To ask a question about this policy, open an issue in the lifekit-hq/.github repository on GitHub, or contact the lifekit-hq organization owner on GitHub.
